Our mission.

Christ's Salvation Church exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and lead people into a saving relationship with Him.

We seek to build a Christ-centered community where individuals and families can grow in faith, worship God, and live according to His Word. We are committed to serving and encouraging our community while extending the love of Christ to others.

Through worship, discipleship, evangelism, fellowship, and community service, we seek to make disciples and reflect Christ in our community and beyond.
